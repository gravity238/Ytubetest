# Brief

## Concept
A second-person, Day One zombie-apocalypse survival story. You begin trapped in your apartment, barricaded behind a locked door. As the power fails, smoke fills the building, and the barricade starts to give way, you are forced onto the fire escape and out into a dangerous street.

## Format
- Platform: YouTube
- Runtime target: 5 minutes
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 landscape
- POV: Second person
- Visual direction: Hand-drawn stick-figure animation inspired by the supplied references: bold, slightly rough black outlines, simple expressive faces, flat restrained colors, readable cinematic staging.
